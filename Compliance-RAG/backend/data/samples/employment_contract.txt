EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This Employment Contract is entered into between Acme GmbH ("Employer") and the Employee.

1. Position and Duties
The Employee is hired as Software Engineer and agrees to perform duties assigned by the Employer.

2. Compensation
The Employee receives a monthly gross salary of EUR 5,500, paid on the last business day of each month.

3. Working Hours
Normal working hours are 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday, in accordance with applicable law.

4. Personal Data Processing
The Employer processes employee personal data for payroll, tax reporting, and performance of this employment contract.
The Employee consents to the processing of personal data necessary for HR administration and may withdraw consent
by written notice to hr@acme.example, subject to legal limitations.

5. Confidentiality
The Employee must keep confidential all business information learned during employment.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate this contract with three months' notice to the end of a calendar month.

7. Governing Law
This contract is governed by the laws of Germany.
